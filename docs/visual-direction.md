# Prototype visual direction

## Locked style

Soft, dreamy children's storybook illustration with gentle dimensional shading. It should read as polished 2D illustration rather than a 3D render.

- Painterly, simplified surfaces rather than realistic fur/cloth/materials.
- Rounded, friendly forms and strong silhouettes.
- Bright but harmonious colour.
- Gentle atmospheric depth and parallax.
- Interactive objects remain clearer and simpler than scenery.
- Visual richness should mostly be painted into assets rather than computed at runtime.

## Ayla Beast

The protagonist is the existing pink/coral Ayla Beast character: large expressive eyes, dark curved horns, large ears, tuft of hair and purple spotted headband. Prototype outfit is pink hoodie, floral skirt and colourful trainers.

## World construction

Do not bake a complete level into one illustration. Build the river from independent reusable sprites/layers:

- distant background
- repeating/scrolling water
- start and far banks
- rocks
- crocodiles
- Ayla
- bicycle/basket
- retrieval objects
- foreground decoration/effects

This allows longer/harder river levels without regenerating a full background.
